f. Biggest flaws

Biggest flaw: The object's position in the camera frame (S3b's track seen through P1c's camera) costs 0.62 AUC on its own (mean of the dev episodes).

Evidence: AUC 1.000 → 0.460 (ep40) / 0.295 (ep26); the track's depth sits 5.5 cm / 3.5 cm (median) from the stereo depth; S0.2's true positions alone would score 0.974 / 0.655; a perfect mesh at the same poses gains only 0.010 / 0.001 AUC.

Second: On ep26 the motion lands on the wrong object, so the best moving candidate scores 0.15 AUC below standing still.

Evidence: best ref_keyframe 0.349 vs still 0.500; plastic_dish_rack moves 12.9 cm in our track and is reached at frame 6, while ground truth has it moving on 0% of frames; white_pot moves 22.5 cm in our track and is never reached, while ground truth has it moving on 35% of frames; S5's frame-0 seating gap 9.4 cm.

Third: The camera drifts against the table while nothing moves, by 4.1 cm on ep26 (P1c's bar is 1 cm).

Evidence: plane-height spread over the rest window 2.3 cm (ep40) / 4.1 cm (ep26); ep26: S0.2's camera common-mode RMS at rest 4.6 cm vs the objects' own 4.6 cm; part of the first flaw; the stand-in camera is the team's DROID path, which v3's stereo-anchored P1c replaces.
