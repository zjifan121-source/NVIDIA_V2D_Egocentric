\subsection{Path A$'$: metric scene refinement (fixed-mesh tracking)}
\label{sec:wentao}
A second Path~A variant, with its own metric front end (FoundationStereo depth on the
rectified pair; SAM~3 text-prompted masks with SAM~2 propagation as fallback; DROID-SLAM with the
background stereo depth as prior and hands and objects masked; WiLoR$\rightarrow$HaMeR MANO hands
with a per-sequence shape and short-gap filling), generates SAM~3D meshes from up to two clear,
complementary views and accepts them only after an explicit structural check; fits one uniform
size per mesh over three support frames with a free SE(3) per frame, validated on two held-out
frames by Pareto selection over IoU, depth residual and coverage; and tracks each object by
\emph{bidirectional} FoundationPose from one reviewed anchor frame on the fixed metric mesh
(10 registration / 5 tracking iterations, no EKF or scale search), one bundle per episode. It can
deform meshes with a 9-parameter part-shape model or a 16-node contact-shape deformation
constrained by silhouettes, depth and observed table contact, and refine poses temporally with
stereo landmarks (corrections capped at 6\,cm); these run as separately frozen candidates and are
recorded per bundle. On public ep27 this cut inter-object RPE from 41.4 to 1.6\,cm (AUC
0.267$\rightarrow$0.913); the retained reconstruction scores 0.908 ADD-AUC over the 20 public
episodes with the official metric code (ground truth used for evaluation only), and on eight
scored sequences an alignment proxy rose from 0.631 to 0.757.

\textbf{Scene hygiene} \cut (last 36\,h; details in Appendix~\ref{app:patha-prime}): hands
re-attached to the final object tracks (spoon-in-hand angle 21$^\circ$ off before); a per-frame
hold rule replacing the exclusive attach that produced false grasps in ep14/15/16/28 (wrist
15--47\,cm from the surface before attachment); cup spin and spoon roll taken from the holding
hand; the support seated at the object's lowest point $-2$\,mm after a frame-0 audit found
1--6\,cm of penetration; the URDF inertial origin moved to the shell centroid (pot 8\,cm off axis,
25$^\circ$ tip at rest); \texttt{njmax} 2048 / \texttt{nconmax} 1024 for nested cups. Two results
transfer: ep28 rebuilt with the observed-size pot, the hold rule and the centre-of-mass fix trains
to 0.914 (DEF, 100M) and 0.930 (0.1\,m / 0.5\,rad termination, 90M) against the base's 0.906 on its
own scene and passes both ground-truth-free checks of Sec.~\ref{sec:selection} (trusted-frame
proxy $+0.012$, emulator $+0.026$, camera $+0.012$; not uploaded, quota), whereas a donor pot 2\,cm
shorter had raised an observation proxy by 0.0145 and still failed at the hand-off (3.5\,cm wrist
gap, pot 0.22). As a control, the base's own ep33 scene trained with our DEF recipe reads 0.361 at
70M against 0.901 for the base's run, so on ep33 the recipe, not the reconstruction, is the gap:
with the 0.1\,m / 0.5\,rad thresholds our ep33 scene went 0.637$\rightarrow$0.827 (spoon
0.30$\rightarrow$0.75).
