\subsection{Path A$'$ details}
\label{app:patha-prime}
\textbf{Stages.} (1)~Ingest and calibration: three-way video decode with frame-index
bookkeeping; the 14-parameter distortion model; the 180$^\circ$ device rotation and the
RGB-to-stereo extrinsics applied together. (2)~Metric depth: FoundationStereo on the stereo pair,
resampled onto the RGB grid with a validity mask (the stereo stream is offset by two frames from
RGB; the first frames share one stereo frame). (3)~Masks: SAM~3 text prompts per object and hand,
SAM~2 propagation as fallback. (4)~Camera: DROID-SLAM with the background stereo depth as prior
and dynamic objects and hands masked out; the table plane is fitted from depth. (5)~Hands: WiLoR
detection and identity, HaMeR MANO fits, FoundationStereo-aligned scale, short-gap filling and one
shape per sequence. (6)~Meshes and size: structure-gated SAM~3D candidates and the shared metric
size of Sec.~\ref{sec:wentao}. (7)~Tracking: fixed-mesh bidirectional FoundationPose from one
reviewed anchor. (8)~Bundle: object poses, meshes with materials, hands, camera, table plane and a
manifest per episode; absolute paths are rewritten on the training host.

\textbf{Selection without the board.} Once the daily Kaggle quota was exhausted, A$'$ candidates
were judged by two instruments that use neither hidden ground truth nor leaderboard probes: a
\emph{board emulator} (the kit's registration, frame-0 alignment and metric code, with the
incumbent's reference track and mesh standing in for the hidden solution) and a \emph{camera
proxy} (the rollout aligned on frame~0, rendered into the scored frames and scored by mask overlap
and depth). On 14 single-episode swaps with known board outcomes their episode deltas correlate
with the board at Spearman 0.956 and 0.912; requiring both to beat the incumbent (``two-check'')
accepted five winners, all correct, rejected all eight losers and missed one winner. Under this
rule the ep28 scene of Sec.~\ref{sec:wentao} trained with the KEN thresholds (0.930 own-reference
AUC at 90M; emulator $+0.026$, camera $+0.012$) was the strongest candidate of the final day; it
was ready after the AUC board's quota had been used up. ep33 with the KEN thresholds (0.827
own-reference AUC) was rejected: its emulator delta is $-0.21$ because the A$'$ cup and spoon
differ in size from the incumbent's (18.7 vs.\ 17.1\,cm and 31.8 vs.\ 27.8\,cm).

\textbf{Artefacts.} A$'$ reconstructions, scenes, rollouts and policies are on the team's

reading back every file's SHA; the scene-consistency tools (hand re-attach, seating, spin, roll
and centre-of-mass fixes, probe review and release builders) are in the team repository.
