
Once the daily Kaggle quota was exhausted, Path~A$'$ candidates were additionally judged by two
instruments that use neither hidden ground truth nor board probes: a \emph{board emulator} (the
kit's registration, frame-0 alignment and metric code, with the incumbent's reference track and
mesh standing in for the hidden solution) and a \emph{camera proxy} (the rollout rendered into
the scored frames and scored by mask overlap and depth). On 14 single-episode swaps with known
board outcomes their episode deltas correlate with the board at Spearman 0.956 and 0.912;
requiring both to beat the incumbent (``two-check'') called five winners, all correct, rejected
all eight losers and missed one.
